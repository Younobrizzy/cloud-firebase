GMAIL SMTP SETUP

Cloudflare Pages variables:

Text:
SMTP_HOST=smtp.gmail.com
SMTP_PORT=465
SMTP_USERNAME=your@gmail.com
SMTP_FROM_NAME=Your Business Name
SMTP_FROM_EMAIL=your@gmail.com
CONTACT_TO=your@gmail.com

Secret:
SMTP_PASSWORD=your 16-character Gmail App Password

EMAIL FLOW

The contact form sends:
POST /api/contact

Cloudflare sends through:
smtp.gmail.com:465

The email headers are:
From: SMTP_FROM_NAME <SMTP_FROM_EMAIL>
To: CONTACT_TO
Reply-To: visitor's email

IMPORTANT

1. Use a Gmail App Password, not your normal Gmail password.
2. Gmail App Passwords require 2-Step Verification.
3. This project uses Gmail implicit TLS on port 465.
4. Keep SMTP_PASSWORD as a Cloudflare Secret.
5. Do not put SMTP credentials in frontend JavaScript or GitHub.
6. SMTP_FROM_EMAIL should normally match the authenticated Gmail account
   (SMTP_USERNAME), unless Gmail has authorized the address as a sending alias.
7. SMTP_FROM_NAME is the name recipients see in their inbox.
